📊 TradingView Supercharts

Three advanced charts with different configurations for comparison

📈 Chart 1: AVGO - Daily (BB 9,2 & SMA 9)

Daily Timeframe: Shows each candlestick representing one trading day. BB(9,2): Bollinger Bands with 9-period length and 2 standard deviations - measures short-term price volatility. The bands expand during volatile periods and contract during consolidation. SMA(9): Simple Moving Average over 9 periods - shows the short-term price trend.

📊 Chart 2: AVGO - Weekly (BB 9,2 & SMA 9)

Weekly Timeframe: Each candlestick represents one week of trading. Better for identifying medium-term trends and swing trading opportunities. BB(9,2): Tracks weekly volatility patterns. SMA(9): 9-week moving average for trend direction.

📉 Chart 3: AVGO - Monthly (BB 9,2 & SMA 9)

Monthly Timeframe: Each candlestick represents one month. Ideal for long-term investment analysis and identifying major trend changes. BB(9,2): Monthly volatility bands for macro perspective. SMA(9): 9-month trend average.

📊 Chart 4: AVGO - 3 Months (BB 9,2 & SMA 9)

3-Month Timeframe: Each candlestick represents 3 months (quarterly). Perfect for analyzing quarterly earnings patterns and seasonal trends. BB(9,2): Quarterly volatility assessment. SMA(9): 9-quarter moving average.

📈 Chart 5: AVGO - 6 Months (BB 9,2 & SMA 9)

6-Month Timeframe: Each candlestick represents 6 months (semi-annual). Great for analyzing long-term investment cycles and major market movements. BB(9,2): Semi-annual volatility bands. SMA(9): 9 semi-annual periods trend.

📉 Chart 6: AVGO - 12 Months (BB 9,2 & SMA)

12-Month Timeframe: Each candlestick represents one full year (annual). Best for multi-decade analysis and understanding the company's historical performance. BB(9,2): Annual volatility for macro-economic cycles. SMA(9): 9-year historical trend.
