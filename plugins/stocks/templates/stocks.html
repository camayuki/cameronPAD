🧠 Ensemble ML Algorithm Explained

📊 Linear Regression (40% weight)

Calculates the best-fit line through historical prices using y = mx + b. Identifies long-term trends and extrapolates them into the future. Best for stable, trending stocks.

📈 Weighted Moving Average (40% weight)

Analyzes short-term (3 days) vs long-term (7 days) momentum to detect trend direction. Applies exponential dampening to avoid over-predicting. Adapts to volatility patterns.

📉 Exponential Moving Average (20% weight)

Gives more weight to recent prices using multiplier = 2/(n+1). Reacts faster to price changes than simple averages. Captures recent market sentiment.

🔢 Final Ensemble Formula:
Final_Prediction = (Linear_Regression × 0.40) + (Weighted_MA × 0.40) + (EMA_Trend × 0.20)
